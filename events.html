QUESTIONS SUPPORTERS ASK
When do hearing outcomes post here?+

After each adjournment, once legal and fact-check review confirms what occurred and what may safely be said. The dedicated post-hearing release carries the full verified status.

Can I show support during the hearings?+

Public access and campaign posture publish here once confirmed. Any presence must follow designated areas and local rules. Never contact judges, jurors, witnesses, or court staff — support the campaign, not the proceedings.

How do local actions get listed?+

Submit through the form above. An organizer-support lead reviews every submission against campaign standards — lawful, nonviolent, accessible — before it appears on this page.

How should I talk about the case?+

Use the approved message bank in the toolkit. Post the real Karmelo, don't share the mugshot, don't spread unverified claims, and keep verified facts distinct from campaign positions.

Why do some listings say “pending”?+

Because we publish nothing before it's confirmed. A pending label means the date is real and details are being verified — check back, or join updates and we'll bring them to you.

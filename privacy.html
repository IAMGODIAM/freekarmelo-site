Privacy on this site

This site collects as little as a campaign can and still function: a signup form you choose to fill in, and standard analytics. Here is all of it, in plain language.

What we collect

Three things, two of them optional

A signup form, aggregate analytics, and the server logs every website has.

The signup form. When you add your name, we receive the name, email address, and city you typed. Nothing is collected until you press the button. Signups are stored in a campaign database operated by E5 Enclave on our own infrastructure.

Analytics. The site runs Google Analytics 4 (property G-05PYDYP5S0), which uses cookies to tell us — in aggregate — which pages are read, on what kind of device, from what approximate region. We use it to understand what the campaign should publish next.

Hosting logs. Our host keeps standard server logs (IP address, page requested, time) as virtually all web hosting does.

There are no advertising trackers on this site, no social-media pixels, and no sale or rental of data to anyone, ever.

How we use it

Updates you asked for, and counting

Signups get campaign updates — hearing dates, events, calls to action. Analytics tells us how many people stood with us.

Every email we send includes a way to stop receiving them. We may report aggregate numbers publicly ("more than a thousand people signed"), never individual names without separate, explicit permission.

Your choices

See it, correct it, delete it

Ask and we act — within 30 days, usually much faster.

To unsubscribe, use the link in any email. To request a copy of what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, write to us through e5enclave.com/contact. Analytics cookies can be blocked with standard browser settings or Google’s own opt-out tools; the site works fine without them.

Third parties

Who else touches the data

Three service providers, each seeing only what its role requires.

Google processes analytics data under its own privacy policy. GitHub Pages serves the site and keeps hosting logs. Cloudflare provides DNS and delivery. None of them receives your signup information for their own use.

Children

A general-audience site

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child submitted the form, contact us and we will delete the entry.

The controller

Who is responsible

E5 Enclave Incorporated — a 501(c)(3) public charity, EIN 99-3822441, Liberty City, Miami, Florida.

Organization-wide privacy practices are published at e5enclave.com/privacy-policy, and more about who we are is on the about page. When this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page changes with it.

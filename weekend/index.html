THE WORLD SHOWED UP.

And it looked like this.

Cities across the world gave a weekend to a teenager they have never met. People painted signs at kitchen tables, stood on corners in the heat, filled sanctuaries, and put his name where it could not be ignored. Every picture on this page was taken by someone who was there.

§ WHAT THIS WEEKEND WAS FOR

IT ISN'T OVER.
THAT'S THE POINT.

The weekend was never the finish line. On August 22 a judge denied Karmelo Anthony a new trial in a half-page order with no explanation, and the 35-year sentence stands. The appeal is now live before the Fifth District Court of Appeals in Dallas — out of the county that produced the jury. No argument date has been set.

What these photographs prove is that the movement did not need a ruling to show up, and will not need one to keep showing up. The letters keep going. The cities are still on the map. The next thing asked of you is smaller than a weekend and it matters just as much.

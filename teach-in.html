RECAP · TEACH-IN NO. 001

August 2 · Pan African Connection · Dallas

No. 001 was a teaching — not a rally, not a press event. Neighbors in a Dallas bookstore full of Black art: the case read plainly from the record, letters written at the tables, and questions answered without spin.

And there was music. An artist sang a prayer over the room — for the whole hood to make it home — and one line stayed with everybody: “Please don’t make me no hashtag or slogan.” That is this work. A young man’s real name, spoken with love — never reduced.

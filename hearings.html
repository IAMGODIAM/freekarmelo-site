THE POST-TRIAL MOTIONS · PLAINLY EXPLAINED

What is being decided —
and what is not.

Two separate motions, two separate days, two separate questions. Neither hearing decides guilt or innocence.

CONFIRMED — COLLIN COUNTY CASE RECORD
WEDNESDAY · AUGUST 19 · 9:00 AM CT

Motion to Recuse

What it asks. The defense asks the court to remove Judge John Roach from any further role in the case, on grounds of alleged bias.

Who presides. Judge Sid Harle of the Fourth Administrative Judicial Region — a different judge — hears and decides this motion.

What is decided that day. Whether the trial judge remains involved going forward. Not guilt, not the verdict, not the sentence.

PRESIDING · JUDGE SID HARLE
FOURTH ADMINISTRATIVE JUDICIAL REGION
CONFIRMED — COLLIN COUNTY CASE RECORD
THURSDAY · AUGUST 20 · 9:00 AM CT

Motion for New Trial

What it asks. The defense asks the court to set aside the conviction and order a new trial.

Who presides. The presiding judge and courtroom for Aug. 20 remain to be assigned.

What is decided that day. Whether a new trial is granted. Not guilt, not the verdict, not the sentence.

THE DEFENSE'S CLAIMS — ATTRIBUTED, NOT SETTLED

The defense maintains that the trial was effectively closed to the public; that the jury instructions hampered the self-defense case; and that the trial judge publicly defended the verdict to reporters. These are the defense's claims, not adjudicated facts.

CONFIRMED: THE MOTION TO RECUSE JUDGE JOHN ROACH IS SET FOR AUG. 19 AT 9:00 A.M.; THE MOTION FOR A NEW TRIAL IS SET FOR AUG. 20 AT 9:00 A.M. IN COLLIN COUNTY. THE AUG. 20 JUDGE AND COURTROOM REMAIN TO BE ASSIGNED.

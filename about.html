The organization behind this campaign

Who runs this site

freekarmelo.net is operated by E5 Enclave Incorporated, a 501(c)(3) public charity based in Liberty City, Miami. This page says plainly who we are, why we do this work, and how to hold us accountable.

The organization

E5 Enclave Incorporated

A Black-led, lineage-led nonprofit think tank founded on 2 June 2024 in Liberty City, Miami — a coalition of the willing doing community work in the open.

E5 Enclave Incorporated is an active 501(c)(3) public charity. Our federal employer identification number is EIN 99-3822441, and our status can be verified directly on the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search or on our disclosures page. Contributions are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

The Enclave runs programs across four fronts: criminal-justice advocacy, including this campaign; food sovereignty through Lineage Farms; community research and open data through the Black Dragons Initiative; and education through the McCartney Academy. The full picture — mission, governance, programs, and financial disclosures — lives on our main site: about the organization, our mission, and our programs.

Why this campaign

What freekarmelo.net does

This site is the public-record arm of our criminal-justice program, focused on the case of Karmelo Anthony — People v. Anthony, 296th District Court, Collin County, Texas.

We do four things here. We keep a documented record of the case as it moves — the conviction of June 9, 2026, the denied motion for a new trial, the appeal now filed — with our positions clearly marked as advocacy. We coordinate solidarity events and publish the materials for them. We provide printable signs, flyers, and teach-in resources for people who want to stand up in their own city. And we collect signatures from supporters who want updates as the appeal proceeds.

Advocacy for due process and for a child in the legal system is core to our charitable mission. Where this site states facts, they come from the court record and published reporting; where it argues, the argument is ours and labeled as such. The organization also maintains a companion record at e5enclave.com/justice/karmelo-anthony.

Money

This site sells nothing

freekarmelo.net does not process donations, run advertising, or sell anything. There is no store and no paid placement here.

The costs of this campaign — hosting, printing, event materials — are carried by E5 Enclave Incorporated. People who want to support the organization can do so at e5enclave.com/donate, where gifts are processed by the organization directly and are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

Your data

What we collect here

One signup form, standard analytics, nothing else. No data is ever sold.

When you add your name, we store the name, email, and city you give us in our own campaign database, and use them to send the updates you asked for. The site also runs Google Analytics so we can see, in aggregate, which pages people read. The complete policy — retention, your rights, third parties — is on the privacy page.

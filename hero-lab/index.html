The Seam

One frame instead of two. Both portraits occupy the same rectangle and you drag the seam between them — so the visitor performs the sentence rather than reading it. His face fills the width instead of sitting at 342px.

B

The Plate

Museum discipline. Full-width stacked on a phone, paired on desktop, and the inert labels become dated, sourced captions. Specific facts carry more feeling than abstract nouns.

What it needs from you: the two bracketed facts. When and where was each photograph taken? A real date under a portrait does more work than any effect on this page.

C

The Hold

Scroll-driven. One frame; as it passes through the viewport the child dissolves into the hero and the line changes with him. Cinematic, and it costs the visitor nothing.
